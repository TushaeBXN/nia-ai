## 3. REVERSE-ENGINEERABLE PATHWAYS
*(mechanisms this network champions that a grassroots mobility initiative can use on its own terms)*

1. **Education Savings Accounts / ECCA scholarships** — the network built the ESA architecture; nothing restricts who applies. Community orgs can become **Scholarship Granting Organizations** or partner with one, directing the new $1,700-credit donor stream to their own students (live Jan 2027; 30 states opted in).
2. **Apprenticeship-first credentialing** — Institute for the American Worker / American Compass materials argue for employer-funded training over college debt. Same argument funds a community pre-apprenticeship pipeline; their published cost-benefit models are reusable in *your* grant applications.
3. **Occupational licensing reform** — ALEC/state-think-tank model bills lower licensing barriers (hours, fees, reciprocity, "fresh start" for people with records). These bills directly help low-income workers enter trades — support them where they appear; Institute for Justice (not on the board, but the litigation leader here) publishes a state-by-state license-burden atlas.
4. **Charter/microschool infrastructure** — Hillsdale-affiliated charter networks and state charter laws create school-founding pathways that Black-led education groups have also used (see: rising Black homeschool rate, ~3x since 2019). The legal scaffolding is neutral; who builds on it decides whom it serves.
5. **Small-issuer capital deregulation** (Reg A/CF streamlining, accredited-investor broadening advocated in ch. 27) — cuts the same legal costs for a community investment cooperative as for anyone else.
6. **Corporate-neutrality trackers** — 1792 Exchange publishes company "bias ratings" pressuring corporate giving; know it exists because it reshapes which corporate philanthropy streams stay available to community nonprofits, and which reframe rather than exit.
7. **Federal-contractor space after EO 11246 revocation** — with affirmative-action reporting gone, *voluntary* supplier-diversity programs at private companies become the remaining channel; National Minority Supplier Development Council certification is private and unaffected by the EO.
