# MODULE 3 — Educational Sovereignty & Skill Building
**PDF schematic — credentials and skills, debt-free where possible, using every pathway now on the table**

## Unit 3.1 — The New Map of School Money (45 min)
- What moved: Title I administration → Dept. of Labor (phase-out proposed but not law); school civil-rights complaints → **DOJ** (and your state agency — dual-file); special education oversight → HHS (IDEA rights themselves unchanged)
- What opened: **ECCA Federal Scholarship Tax Credit — live Jan 1, 2027.** $1,700-credit donations fund Scholarship Granting Organizations; families up to 300% of area median income qualify; 30 states opted in
- Claim path taught step-by-step: check governor opt-in → find in-state SGOs → apply early → eligible expenses go far beyond tuition (tutoring, transportation, tech, special-needs services, homeschool materials)
- State ESAs where they exist: what's covered, application calendars, receipt discipline
- **Guardrails lesson:** what your public school still owes every child regardless (IDEA, McKinney-Vento homeless education rights, free meals where enrolled)

## Unit 3.2 — The Apprenticeship Superhighway (90 min — the heart of the module)
- Why this path is *growing* while loan programs shrink — and why that's usable
- **Earn-while-you-learn math:** union apprenticeships (IBEW electrician, UA plumbers/pipefitters, carpenters, sheet metal) pay from day one with raises every 6–12 months, ending journeyman ~$70–100k+ with zero debt
- Finding programs: apprenticeship.gov finder · union locals' application windows · community-college pre-apprenticeships · high-school youth apprenticeship (new grants pending in WIOA reauthorization)
- **Quality check taught:** Registered Apprenticeship listing = quality floor; for any "industry-recognized" (IRAP-style) program demand published completion + wage outcomes before enrolling
- Benefits interplay: enrolled training hours **satisfy SNAP/Medicaid work requirements** — enrollment letter template included
- Beyond construction: healthcare (CNA→LPN→RN ladders, employer-paid), IT (registered tech apprenticeships), advanced manufacturing, transit/utilities

## Unit 3.3 — College Without the Trap (60 min)
- Pell (intact) + community college transfer pathways = cheapest BA route; HBCUs' role and the block-grant changes to watch
- Student loans in the Treasury-transfer era: download your **full StudentAid.gov file today**, screenshot payment counts, recertify PSLF/IDR annually, dispute errors via servicer + CFPB portal + state AG
- New repayment reality for new borrowers (two-plan structure) — decision tree included
- Employer tuition benefits (§127, up to $5,250/yr tax-free) and apprenticeship-to-degree credit articulation
- **First-gen support:** TRIO's agency moved — services are still legally owed; how to insist in writing; college-access nonprofits as backup

## Unit 3.4 — Building Your Own Education Infrastructure (45 min)
- Microschools, learning pods, homeschool co-ops: legal basics per state, ECCA/ESA funding eligibility, the rising Black homeschool movement's playbook
- Free/cheap credentialing stack: library card resources, state workforce board-funded certificates (WIOA ITAs — CareerOneStop finder), employer certificates with wage data
- Occupational licensing: reforms lowering hours/fees/reciprocity; "fresh start" laws for people with records — state-by-state check
- Community skill exchange: church/community-center classes as pre-apprenticeship feeders
