# MODULE 2 — Economic Resilience & Capital Acquisition
**PDF schematic — building income and capital that doesn't depend on any one program surviving**

## Unit 2.1 — Claim Every Dollar Already Yours (45 min)
- The four-figure annual event: **EITC (up to ~$7,800) + Child Tax Credit + Saver's Credit**, filed free at VITA sites (irs.gov/vita) — 1 in 5 eligible workers never claims EITC
- Amend the last 3 years if you missed credits
- Benefits screening in one sitting: benefits.gov + 211 — *then* treat benefits as a bridge, not the plan, because work requirements and time limits are tightening (SNAP ABAWD now to age 64, 80 documented hrs/month; Medicaid community-engagement phasing in; HUD term limits proposed)
- **The compliance-as-strategy move:** approved *training programs count as work hours* — enroll in the WIOA/apprenticeship program that satisfies today's requirement AND builds the exit

## Unit 2.2 — Banking, Credit, and Predator Defense (60 min)
- Credit as infrastructure: FCRA dispute process (Nia's existing credit-law curriculum), secured cards, credit-builder loans at credit unions
- **The referee is off the field:** CFPB enforcement largely paused — its complaint portal still works (use it; companies must respond), but PREVENTION is now the defense: the 5 predatory-loan tells, state usury caps, state AG complaint lines
- Where to bank: credit unions + CDFI banks (mission-bound, Black-owned bank list maintained in resource directory)

## Unit 2.3 — The Capital Ladder for a Small Business (90 min)
Sequence taught as a ladder — each rung qualifies you for the next:
1. **Formalize free:** EIN (irs.gov, free), business checking, simple bookkeeping (6 clean months of statements = your first "credit history")
2. **Microcapital:** SBA Microloan (≤$50k via nonprofit intermediaries, character-based) · **CDFIs** (ofn.org locator — 1,400+ lenders whose mission is you) · Kiva 0% microloans · community foundation grants
3. **State rails:** SSBCI programs (state-administered credit support), state minority/small-business programs where they survive — current names checked per state
4. **Guaranteed lending (survived the restructuring):** SBA 7(a) + 504 through banks/credit unions — SBA *direct* lending is ended, so the bank relationship from rung 1 is the door
5. **Community capital:** investment clubs; **Reg CF crowdfunding** (FINRA portals) — deregulation in this area cuts the legal cost of raising money from your own community; co-op structures
- Free help that still exists: SBDCs, SCORE mentors, community college small-business centers
- **Case studies:** home childcare business (licensed, meets Unit 2.1 work rules, serves the community); trades LLC after apprenticeship (Module 3 handoff); NMSDC supplier certification → corporate contracts (private program, unaffected by federal EO changes)

## Unit 2.4 — Independent Work Without Getting Stripped (45 min)
- Gig/contractor status: the classification rules loosened — know what you give up (overtime, unemployment insurance, workers' comp, employer FICA)
- Misclassification tells + wage-claim path: DOL WHD 1-866-487-9243 (status-blind) and state labor agencies
- If genuinely independent: quarterly taxes, SEP-IRA/solo 401(k), health coverage options, LLC basics — "you are now your own benefits department; here is the checklist"

## Unit 2.5 — Household Resilience Plan (30 min)
- The one-page plan: income streams (aim for 2+), claimed credits, emergency fund at a credit union, documented benefits file, capital-ladder rung and next rung
- Family/collective versions: sou-sou/susu formalized, investment club charter template
